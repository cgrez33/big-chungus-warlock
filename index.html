Profession roadmap

Levels 1–30
Herbalism + Skinning

Gather herbs as you quest, skin mobs you already kill, sell leather and bank your herbs. Level First Aid with your cloth.

Around level 30 onward
Herbalism + Alchemy

Drop Skinning when you have enough materials and gold to batch craft. Keep First Aid current and add Cooking/Fishing opportunistically.
