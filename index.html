Quick sorting rules

Usually vendor

Gray loot, low-value single scraps, and materials whose realistic auction profit is smaller than postage and auction costs.

Check AH first

Full stacks of leather, herbs, cloth, uncommon gathering drops, green equipment, and anything with a meaningful price premium.

Send to bank alt

Profitable stacks and valuable items when you already pass a mailbox. Combine stacks into fewer attachments to reduce postage.

Hold selectively

Some rare early gathering materials might appreciate later, but that's speculative. Don't tie up all your training silver in inventory.
